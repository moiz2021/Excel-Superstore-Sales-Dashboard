# 📌 Project Overview

This interactive Microsoft Excel Dashboard analyzes retail sales, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance from 2021 to 2024. Built using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and dynamic Slicers, this dashboard allows decision-makers to filter data seamlessly across product categories, years, and months to uncover actionable business insights.

## 📈 Key Visuals & Analytical Insights

### Profit by Year & Category (Clustered Column Chart):

- Compares annual profitability across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology from 2021 to 2024.

## Insight: Technology and Office Supplies consistently generate the highest profit margins, peaking in 2023 (~$40K for Technology), while Furniture maintains noticeably lower profitability.

### Sales by Sub-Category (Horizontal Bar Chart):

- Ranks all product sub-categories by total revenue.

- Insight: Phones and Chairs/Binders lead revenue generation (approaching $300K), whereas Labels and Fasteners contribute the least.

### Top 5 Customers by Profit (Column Chart):

### Sales by States (Treemap Chart):

- Visualizes geographic revenue concentration across US states.

- Insight: California dominates overall sales volume, followed by New York, Texas, and Washington.

### Sales by Months with Trendline (Column + Linear Trend Chart):

- Tracks monthly revenue seasonality from January to December.

- Insight: Sales show a strong upward linear trajectory throughout the year, with major spikes in March, September, November, and peaking in December (~$240K).

### Customer Count by Year (Donut Chart):

- Displays the annual distribution of active customers:

- 2023: 31% (Highest customer engagement)

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

1- Dynamic Slicers: Filter the entire dashboard in real-time by Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology), Year (2021–2024), and Month (01–12).

2- Custom Number Formatting: Configured Pivot Table values to display large figures cleanly in thousands (0K) for improved readability.

3- Structured Workbook: Organized cleanly with dedicated tabs (Data, Top 5 Customer, Sales by state, sales by month, yearly count, and Dashboard).

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

1- Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, structuring, and dashboard layout design.

2- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts: Multi-dimensional data aggregation and visualization.

3- Slicers & Report Connections: Synchronized filtering across multiple Pivot Charts.

4- Custom Number Formatting: Automated K (thousands) axis scaling.
